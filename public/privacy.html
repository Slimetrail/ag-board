Privacy Policy

Ag See a Need Fill a Need (“the board,” “we”) is a South Carolina farm and neighbor board. This policy explains what we collect and how we use it. Last updated: September 4, 2026.

Who this covers

People who browse the board, create an account, post listings, leave notes, or use the Android app that opens this same website.

Information we collect

  • Account info you provide: email, password (stored hashed), display/farm name, and optional profile details.
  • Listings and messages you post on the board (text, photos you upload, county/location you choose).
  • Technical data needed to run the service: session cookies, approximate device/browser info, and standard server logs.

How we use it

We use this information to run the board: sign you in, show listings, let neighbors contact each other through the app, keep the service secure, and improve it. We do not sell your personal information.

Sharing

Listing content you publish is visible to other users. We use hosting and database providers (such as Vercel and Neon) to operate the service. We may disclose information if required by law.

Children

The board is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.

Retention and your choices

You can request account or listing changes by contacting us. We keep account and listing data while your account is active and as needed to operate the board.
